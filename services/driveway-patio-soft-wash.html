Driveway & Patio Soft Wash

Driveways and patios take the brunt of monsoon dust and haboob storms — silt settles into concrete and pavers and builds up fast between cleanings.

We soft wash driveways and patios to lift embedded dirt and staining without the surface damage that comes with high-pressure equipment on concrete or pavers.

Pricing depends on the size of the area — we’ll confirm the exact scope and price when we quote your property.
