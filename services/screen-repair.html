Service

Screen Repair & Replacement

From one torn screen to a full-home rescreen, we repair and replace window and door screens so they look tight and last.

Arizona UV exposure accelerates screen wear far faster than in most climates — mesh that would last a decade elsewhere can go brittle and tear here in a few years. If your screens are looking sun-damaged, that’s expected, not a sign you did anything wrong.

This is also the service most tied to HOA compliance: many Phoenix Valley HOAs send compliance letters every spring calling out torn or missing screens. We can get ahead of that before the letter shows up.
