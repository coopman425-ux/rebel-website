Privacy Policy

REBEL Home Exterior Detailing Solutions LLC
Last updated: October 21, 2026

REBEL Home Exterior Detailing Solutions LLC ("REBEL," "we," "us") provides exterior cleaning and detailing services in Arizona. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, email, service address, and details about the service you want. You give us this when you request a quote, book, or talk with our team.
  • Service records: quotes, appointments, photos of the work we do at your property, payments, and notes about your service.
  • Payment information: if you pay by card, the card is processed by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full card numbers.
  • Website information: basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited. This may be collected through cookies or analytics tools.

How we use your information

  • To give quotes, schedule and perform services, and send reminders
  • To process payments and send receipts
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support
  • To follow up on service quality and offer future services you may want
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the business (for example, payment processing, scheduling and texting software, email, and hosting). They may use it only to provide those services to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not share or sell your mobile number or SMS opt-in consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging (SMS) terms

  • Program: REBEL Home Exterior Detailing Solutions customer messages.
  • What you'll receive: quote follow-ups, appointment confirmations and reminders, "on my way" notices, service follow-ups, receipts, and occasional service offers.
  • How you opt in: by checking the SMS box on our booking form, or by asking our team to text you.
  • Frequency: varies, usually a few messages per job or service visit.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time. You'll get one confirmation text, and then no more messages unless you opt in again.
  • Help: reply HELP, or contact us at (206) 316-7150 or rebel.detail.business@gmail.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Consent to receive texts is not a condition of buying any service.

How long we keep information

We keep customer and service records as long as needed to provide service and meet legal and tax requirements. After that, we delete or anonymize them.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your personal information, or stop marketing messages, by contacting us below. We may keep records we're required to keep by law.

Children

Our services and website are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
