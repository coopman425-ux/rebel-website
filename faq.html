Soft Wash vs. Pressure Washing

What’s the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure paired with the right cleaning solution to lift dirt and organic growth. Pressure washing uses high-force water alone. We default to soft wash almost everywhere because it’s gentler on the surface — and reserve pressure washing for durable surfaces built to take the extra force.

Is pressure washing safe for my roof, driveway, or stucco?

Not always. High-pressure washing can crack roof tiles and shingles and damage concrete or painted stucco when it’s used on the wrong surface. That’s exactly why we soft wash roofs and exteriors by default.

Windows & Screens

How often should I get my windows cleaned in Arizona?

More often than you would in a milder climate. Phoenix Valley monsoon season brings dust and haboob storms that coat glass in fine silt, so most homes here need window cleaning on a shorter cycle — we’ll give you a straight read on cadence when we quote your home.

Why do my window screens wear out so fast here?

Arizona UV exposure accelerates screen wear far faster than in most climates — mesh that would last a decade elsewhere can go brittle and tear here in just a few years. That’s normal, not a sign you did anything wrong.

What’s included in a screen repair or rescreen?

Anything from a single torn screen to a full-home rescreen — see Screen Repair & Replacement for real pricing by scope.

Roof Cleaning

What causes the dark streaks on my roof?

Almost always Gloeocapsa Magma — a common algae growth that shows up as dark streaking, especially on north-facing roofs, driven by Phoenix’s monsoon-season humidity. It’s a real, common issue here, not a sign of a bad roof.

How often should a Phoenix Valley roof be soft washed?

Most homes benefit from a soft wash every year or two, more often on north-facing roofs prone to algae growth from monsoon humidity. Pricing depends on square footage and pitch — we’ll walk through both when we quote the job.

Weather & Timing

When is the best time to book exterior cleaning in Phoenix?

Monsoon season (roughly June through September) is when homes here get dirtiest fastest, as dust and haboob storms build up on windows, screens, and exteriors. Booking before and after monsoon season generally gets the most mileage out of a cleaning.
