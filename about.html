Why soft wash

The method matters as much as the visit

Most exterior cleaning damage doesn’t come from dirt — it comes from the wrong amount of pressure on the wrong surface. High-pressure washing can crack roof tiles and shingles and damage concrete. REBEL uses soft washing — low pressure paired with the right cleaning solution — everywhere that method calls for it, and reserves pressure washing for the surfaces built to take it.

  • Soft wash, not just pressure wash. Our low-pressure method lifts dirt and organic growth without cracking roof tiles, shingles, or concrete the way high-pressure washing can — a real technical choice, not a marketing line.
  • Built for Phoenix, specifically. Monsoon dust and haboob storms, Gloeocapsa Magma algae on north-facing roofs, HOA compliance letters every spring, UV that wears out screens faster than almost anywhere else — we deal with these exact problems every week.
  • Already in the neighborhood. REBEL works block-by-block across the Valley, which means fast turnaround and a crew that knows the area — not a truck dispatched from across town.
